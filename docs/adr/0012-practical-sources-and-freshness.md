# ADR 0012: Practical sources and freshness for crew-facing knowledge

> 中文摘要：为船员/渔民扩展实用来源（海况预警、安全、渔具、鱼种、补贴、市场、港口服务），用 A/B/C/D 权威级、许可证证据和 TTL 时效约束；不训练预报模型、不发布原始环境数据。

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-12

## Context

The register is strong on law, regulation, notice, standard and official
statistics, but the people the knowledge base is for are crew and fishers. The
acceptance instrument contains 30 technical-extension questions, 51 safety
questions and 34 fishery-remote-sensing questions, and the fourth holdout
showed the binding constraint is corpus coverage rather than summary quality.
Practical content is thin, and the current source classes have no vocabulary
for a weather bulletin, a market price or a port-service page.

## Decision

- Extend the source taxonomy with practical classes:
  `practical_guide`, `forecast_bulletin`, `market_data`, `training_material`,
  `port_service`.
- Authority rules for practical claims:
  - tier A/B official pages and national standards may be primary for
    operational advice, safety rules, subsidies and official statistics;
  - tier C municipal pages are a local supplement and are labelled with their
    level;
  - tier D non-official pages (association, university, extension station) are
    background or definition only, never the sole support for an operational
    rule.
- Every practical source records a licence evidence field, a sensitivity, a
  cadence and a `ttl_days` value. A weather or warning bulletin is short-lived;
  a standard or a law is long-lived.
- Weather, sea state and disaster content is cited from the **official
  bulletin**, never generated by the project. The project does not train or
  publish forecast models, raw environmental grids, AIS/VMS tracks or
  full vessel tracks.
- Market prices and port-service information carry the publisher and the
  publication time on the face of the answer; a price without a date is not a
  citable fact.
- New sources enter through the candidate queue after a survey that records
  reachability, parseability, licence evidence and freshness. Unknown licence
  or sensitivity holds the source.

## Consequences

- The corpus becomes broader and more useful to crew, but the licence and
  authority review cost rises; the candidate queue and the private release gate
  must stay strict.
- Freshness becomes a per-source property, not a global policy; the answer
  contract must show as-of and stale labels.
- The public repository still contains no payload, no market price and no
  bulletin text; only metadata, schema and documentation are public.
- Scope creep toward a forecasting platform is explicitly fenced off; a request
  for model output is out of scope and is answered by a bulletin citation or a
  refusal with a channel.

## Alternatives considered

- **Stay policy-only**: higher authority per source, but fails the worker-facing
  purpose; rejected.
- **Scrape news and aggregators**: fast coverage, but not citable and often
  unlicensed; rejected (see the source standard).
- **Use the ephemeral web layer as the practical corpus**: no reproducibility
  and no review; rejected; web leads are promoted through the candidate queue.
