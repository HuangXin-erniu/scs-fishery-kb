# Knowledge Review — 2026-11-06

> **中文摘要**：本次复核覆盖 120 条草稿条款事实、34 条草稿术语和 15 个 playbook。按“有章节定位、有规范语句、来源为 A/B 且许可公开、证据可逐字回查”的标准，晋升 20 条条款事实；其余保留草稿或标记不晋升。34 条术语均未在已登记语料中出现，全部保留草稿并列为来源缺口。修正 6 处 playbook 表述。**这是 AI 辅助初筛，不是人工签字；正式发布前仍需项目所有者抽查。**

## 1. Scope and method

- Facts: 120 draft provision facts in `configs/facts/provisions.yaml`.
- Terms: 34 draft entries in `configs/terms.yaml`.
- Playbooks: 15 playbooks / 32 steps in `configs/playbooks/playbooks.yaml`.
- Reviewer: `agent-review` (AI-assisted first pass), 2026-11-06.
- Promotion criteria: exact verbatim evidence in the registered chunk; source
  authority tier A/B; public or CC-BY-NC-SA-3.0-IGO licence; a section-level
  article or numbered item; a normative sentence (应当/不得/可以/处罚/许可/申请/
  条件/期限/负责); no OCR artifacts, fragments, short text, purpose clauses or
  generic responsibility clauses.
- Non-promoted items stay `draft`; nothing is deleted. The review block records
  `decision` (`promote` / `keep_draft` / `reject`), reviewer, date and reason.

## 5. What this review does not establish

- It is not a human legal review. The owner should spot-check at least the 20
  promoted rows and the six corrected playbook statements.
- It does not rebuild the v1 external fact index. `fact_documents` would index
  100 approved facts if rebuilt; no rebuild is part of this review.
- It does not approve any term. The 34 gaps need sources first.
- It does not change the answer path, service, retrieval, web, index or eval code.
